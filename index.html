PAIRING METHOD

Built on Swiss principles

Swiss: Round 1 is seeded by FIDE rating, highest to lowest. Later rounds prioritize same-score opponents, avoid rematches, and use rating order as a stable tie-break. Colours are assigned to balance each player’s White and Black games. An odd field gets one bye, awarded to a low-ranked player who has not had one. Use a player menu to skip an upcoming round or withdraw.

Round robin: A rotating schedule pairs every player against every other player once. For an odd field, each player receives one scheduled bye. Both formats award 1 point for a win, ½ for a draw, and 0 for a loss. A forfeit awards the point to the opponent but does not count as a played game for colour balance. Final ties use Buchholz (Swiss) or Sonneborn–Berger (round robin), then rating.

FIDE’s 2026 rules require declared rounds, rating-based initial order, score groups, no repeat opponents, and color balancing. Known round absences score 0, and withdrawn players are no longer paired. This app uses those core ideas; its pairing engine is designed for local club use and is not FIDE-certified or a substitute for an approved FIDE pairing program.
